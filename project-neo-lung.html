NEO LUNG

AI Diagnostic Research

NEO LUNG is an AI-assisted lung cancer diagnostic study focused on identifying patterns associated with NSCLC and building a more structured, technology-driven evaluation workflow. The project brings together image classification and risk-based reasoning to support early-stage clinical decision support.

The goal of the system was not only to classify data, but also to create a stronger framework for understanding patient risk. By combining deep learning with a multimodal scoring strategy and an NLP retrieval chatbot, the project explored how AI can support medical professionals with faster, more informed information gathering.

Key highlights

  • Developed a lung cancer diagnostic model using transfer learning approaches.
  • Focused on NSCLC-related risk scoring and structured data interpretation.
  • Created a retrieval-based chatbot to respond to medical inquiry using information-rich workflows.
  • Designed to support not just prediction but deeper analysis and contextual understanding.
