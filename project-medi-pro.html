Project detail

MediPro

Healthcare AI

MediPro is a medical AI project aimed at improving diagnostic support through image analysis and intelligent response systems. The project focuses on chest CT scan classification using deep learning and a supporting multimodal medical risk scoring engine.

The platform was designed to combine data-driven medical understanding with practical user support. Its goal was to help process chest CT image data more efficiently, support risk assessment, and provide a simplified way to answer frequent clinical questions through conversational retrieval.

Key highlights

  • Used MobileNetV2 transfer learning for chest CT image classification.
  • Reached 96% classification accuracy in training and evaluation tasks.
  • Designed a multimodal risk scoring mechanism to support decision-making.
  • Integrated an NLP-based retrieval chatbot for NSCLC medical inquiries.
  • Focused on making AI support more useful in real healthcare scenarios.
